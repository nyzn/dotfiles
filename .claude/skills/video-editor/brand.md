- **Font:** Futura Bold (built into macOS, `/System/Library/Fonts/Supplemental/Futura.ttc`). Fallback where Futura is missing (Linux / cloud): Inter Bold, then DejaVu Sans Bold.
- **Captions:** uppercase, white text, black outline 6px, active word yellow; lower third (MarginV ≈ 25% of height on 9:16). Size ~80px at 1080x1920.
- **Accents / lower-thirds / CTA cards:** green background, yellow or white text.
- **Voice:** casual, direct, short sentences. (BJJ training content.)
